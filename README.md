> [!IMPORTANT]
> NMRiH2 is eight-player co-op with **public matchmaking**. That means the people in your lobby are usually strangers who did not agree to any of this.
>
> **Host-only mode ships enabled** and blocks anything marked **`squad`** while you are a guest. Use this in private lobbies with friends. Dropping into a public server with infinite ammo and a wallhack takes the game from seven people who queued for scarcity and dread.

## What it does

No More Room in Hell 2 is about scarcity — six bullets, one bandage, and a friend who just got bitten and has not told anyone yet. Remove that and there is not much game left, which is why most of this menu is tagged and most of it is off by default.

What it is genuinely good for: **private runs with friends**, learning a map layout before you take it seriously, and the **item highlighter** when the group has spent forty minutes looking for one keycard the procedural layout hid behind a filing cabinet.

## Hotkeys

`Insert` opens the menu · `End` resets everything · `F1`–`F12` as above, all rebindable

> [!TIP]
> The setup that keeps the game intact: **item and objective highlight** plus **revive downed teammates**, and nothing else. The first fixes a procedural-generation frustration, the second helps the people you are playing with. Both leave the scarcity alone.

> [!WARNING]
> **Early Access.** NMRiH2 patches frequently and every patch can move memory offsets, so options fail independently. Read-only mode shows live values without writing anything — use it after an update to check what still works.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Will I get banned?</summary>
There is no PvP and no ranked mode. Whether a server admin removes you is a different question, and in a public lobby it would be a fair one.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use it in public matchmaking?</summary>
Technically yes, and please do not. Seven strangers queued for a scarcity survival game. Private lobby, friends who know.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does no infection stop me turning on my team?</summary>
Yes, and that removes one of the game's better moments. It is client-side, so nobody else is affected either way, but it is worth knowing what you are switching off.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Options stopped working after an update.</summary>
Expected in Early Access. Check the Releases page for a build matching your game version.
</details>
